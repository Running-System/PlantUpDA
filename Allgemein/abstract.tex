	\subsection*{Abstract}

    This diploma thesis presents Plant Up!, a smart gardening system developed to support everyday plant care through objective measurement rather than guesswork. The central objective was to reduce common care mistakes by replacing subjective judgement with measurable environmental data and understandable digital feedback.

    To achieve this, the project was structured into four connected areas: sensor-based data acquisition, edge and cloud communication, backend architecture, and user engagement through gamification. A prototype sensor station was implemented using affordable consumer-grade hardware to measure soil moisture, electrical conductivity, temperature, humidity, and light. These measurements are transmitted through the backend infrastructure and presented in a mobile application, where they are converted into practical care support for the user.

    The work shows that a functional smart-gardening concept can be realized without laboratory equipment or a closed commercial ecosystem. The most important finding is that useful plant monitoring does not depend on expensive specialized hardware, but on a coherent combination of affordable sensors, reliable data processing, and clear presentation of the results. In this way, Plant Up! turns raw environmental measurements into support that can be used in everyday decision-making.

    In conclusion, the project provides a viable foundation for a complete plant-care assistance system that combines measurement, data processing, and user interaction in one solution. Future work could extend the system through broader plant-specific calibration, more advanced recommendation logic, and long-term field testing under real household conditions.
\end{flushleft}
