\section{Conclusion}

This final chapter synthesizes the outcomes of the project, evaluates the overall success of the ``Plant Up!'' system against its initial objectives, and suggests potential areas for future enhancement and larger-scale integration.

\subsection{Summary of Results}
\begin{itemize}
    \item System works reliably.
    \item Plants have a "voice" now.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Evaluation of Technological Potential}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Very cheap (< 15 Euro).
    \item Open Data (Privacy friendly).
    \item Scalable for many plants.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Outlook on Future Developments}
\begin{itemize}
    \item AI Camera for pest detection.
    \item Solar panel for infinite power.
    \item Smart Home integration (Alexa).
\end{itemize}
